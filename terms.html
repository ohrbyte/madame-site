Terms & conditions.

Last updated 16 July 2026

1. Who we are

Madame is a cleaning booking service. When you book through this site you are entering an agreement with us to arrange a cleaner on your behalf. These terms set out what you can expect from us and what we need from you.

2. Booking a clean

A booking is confirmed once you receive a confirmation from us. Until then, the slot you selected is held but not guaranteed. We will always tell you the price before you confirm, and that price will not change afterwards unless the job you described differs materially from the job we find.

3. Changes and cancellations

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your clean. Inside 24 hours we may charge up to 50% of the booking value, because your cleaner has already set that time aside. If we need to cancel on you, you pay nothing and we will find you another slot as a priority.

4. Access

We need to be able to get in. If your cleaner cannot access the property at the agreed time and we cannot reach you within 30 minutes, the visit is treated as a late cancellation.

5. Payment

Payment is taken after the clean is complete. Gift vouchers are redeemed at the time of booking and are valid for 12 months from purchase.

6. Our cleaners

Every cleaner is interviewed, referenced and insured. They are not your employees, and we ask that you do not offer them work directly outside the platform during your time with us or for six months afterwards.

7. If something goes wrong

Tell us within 48 hours and we will put it right — usually by sending someone back at no cost. If something is damaged, we are insured, and we will handle the claim with you rather than leaving you to it.

8. Your data

We keep what we need to run your bookings and nothing more. We do not sell it. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to hello@madame.com.

9. Getting in touch

Questions about any of this are welcome and go to a real person: hello@madame.com.
